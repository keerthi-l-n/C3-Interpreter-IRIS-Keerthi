C3 second-tier interpreter.

For a newborn screen with raised propionylcarnitine (C3). Enter the second-tier results from the same dried blood spot to see which branch of the proposed decision path applies: propionic acidemia (PA), isolated methylmalonic acidemia (MMA), or a B12-related cause.

    03Reference

    Where this ratio sits among published patients.

    PA groups and samples MMA-type groups, incl. B12-related This sample

    30 published rows from Hu 2021, Monostori 2017 and Dubland 2021: group medians and single samples, newborn and on-treatment. No published patient falls between 0.87 and 10.3.

    04Method

    How the cut-offs were set and tested.

    30 / 30published rows sorted correctly into PA vs MMA-type by the ratio
    10 / 10rows with homocysteine sorted correctly into isolated vs B12-related MMA
    100%accuracy of the ratio in each study when its cut-off was learned from the other two
    12× vs 1.3×gap between PA and MMA-type groups: ratio vs methylmalonic acid alone
    • The ratio was chosen because a genome-scale model of human metabolism (Human-GEM) predicted it stays reliable in milder disease, where methylmalonic acid alone does not.
    • The cut-offs of 1 and 10 were read from the same published data they were checked on. They have not been tested on individual patients.
    • Mild B12 deficiency can give near-normal values and may pass step 01.

    ⚠ Not for clinical use. This is a research prototype. A diagnosis needs confirmatory testing by a metabolic specialist.

    Research marker, not used here: the model also predicts that formate separates B12 deficiency from genetic MMA. Formate rises in B12-deficient rats, but it has not been measured for this purpose in patients.
